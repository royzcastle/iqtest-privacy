IQ Test Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026 · Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to IQ Test ("the App"). Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information the App processes, how it is handled, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

2. Information We Collect

The App requires no sign-up and does not collect personal identifying information such as your name, email address, phone number or location.

  • Your test scores and history are stored locally on your device only and are never uploaded to any server;
  • Uninstalling the App or clearing its data permanently removes all local records.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services

The App displays ads served by Google AdMob, which supports the App's free availability:

  • AdMob may collect device identifiers, coarse location (based on IP) and app interaction data in order to serve and measure advertisements;
  • In the EEA and the UK, a Google consent management form (UMP) appears on first launch, letting you freely choose whether personalized ads are allowed;
  • You may reset your advertising identifier or opt out of ad personalization at any time (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad serving is configured as non-child-directed in line with Google's advertising policies.

5. Permissions

  • Nearby devices / network access: used only in development builds to connect to a local development server; the released production build does not use this permission;
  • The App does not access your photos, contacts, microphone or camera.

6. Your Rights

Since all test records are stored locally, you can delete them completely at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data. For advertising-related data, please exercise your rights through Google's privacy policy linked above.

7. Changes to This Policy

Updates to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
