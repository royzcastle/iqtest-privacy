IQ Test Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy applies to IQ Test on both Android and iOS. If translations differ, the English version prevails.

1. Introduction

IQ Test ("the App") is a self-assessment puzzle app. We do not operate a backend service, and the App requires no account, sign-up or login. This policy explains what the App stores on your device and what the advertising SDK may process.

2. Information stored on your device

We do not collect personal identifying information such as your name, email address, phone number or precise location, and we do not receive your test answers or scores.

  • Test results and history are stored locally on your device only and are never uploaded to any server;
  • Your language preference is stored locally so the App opens in the language you chose;
  • Uninstalling the App or clearing its data permanently removes all local records.

3. Advertising and third-party services

Current version: ad-free. This release does not display any advertisements, does not initialize the AdMob SDK and does not show a consent form. The AdMob SDK is bundled in the app package for a possible future update; that update will come with a revised policy and a refreshed data-safety declaration before any ad is shown.

The App is free. Apart from the AdMob SDK described above, the App contains no analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDK.

  • When ads are enabled in a future version, AdMob may process your advertising identifier, IP-based coarse location, device information and ad interaction data to serve and measure advertisements. This data goes to Google under Google's Privacy Policy, not to us;
  • In the EEA, the UK and other regions where consent is required, a Google consent form (UMP) will then be shown before ads begin, so you can choose whether personalized ads are allowed;
  • You can reset or opt out of ad personalization at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

4. Permissions

  • Internet and network state are used solely to load and measure advertisements. The App itself makes no other network requests and sends no test data;
  • Advertising ID (Android AD_ID) is used by AdMob for ad delivery and measurement, subject to your consent choices;
  • The App does not access your photos, contacts, microphone, camera, files or precise GPS location. Additional low-risk permissions declared by the underlying system libraries are not used to collect personal data.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad requests are configured as non-child-directed in line with Google's advertising policies.

6. Your rights

  • Because all test records stay on your device, you can delete them completely at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data;
  • Under GDPR you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your data and withdraw consent at any time. We hold no copy of your data, so deletion is performed on your device; consent for personalized ads can be withdrawn as described in section 3;
  • We do not sell personal information. If you are in a US state with a "Do Not Sell or Share" right, choosing non-personalized ads in the consent form prevents sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights;
  • For advertising data held by Google, please use Google's privacy controls or contact us using the address below.

7. Data retention and security

All App data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. As no data is transmitted to us, there is no server-side retention period and no third-party data processor other than Google AdMob.

8. Changes to this policy

Updates will be published on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will also be reflected in the App's store listing where required.

9. Contact

Questions or requests about this policy: royzcastle@gmail.com · Terms of Service
