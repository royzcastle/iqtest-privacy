Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. IQ Test works fully offline. You can take the test, see your score, and review your results anywhere — no Wi-Fi or cellular data required.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is needed. The app does not ask for your name, email, or any personal information. Your progress is stored only on your own device.

Are there ads?

The current version is completely ad-free. We do not show advertisements and we do not track you.

What data does the app collect?

None. The app collects no user data — no analytics, no identifiers, no location. Your answers and results never leave your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How is my IQ score calculated?

The test dynamically generates questions drawn from several cognitive domains (logical reasoning, pattern recognition, verbal, and numerical). Your raw score is mapped to a standardized IQ scale with a mean of 100 and a standard deviation of 15. Results are for entertainment and self-assessment, not a clinical diagnosis.

I found a bug / I have a suggestion

Please email royzcastle@gmail.com with a short description and, if possible, your device model and iOS version. Screenshots help a lot.

How do I reset my progress?

Uninstalling and reinstalling the app clears all local data. There is no separate reset button because the app keeps nothing on our servers.
